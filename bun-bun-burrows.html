Bun Bun Burrows

A Journey Full of Wonder

You take the role of a kid exploring the garden surrounding his home, defending it from the bunnies trying to eat it all up! Armed with only your trusty water pistol you must not only fight your way through the hoards, you must think your way through them as well. Take advantage of the many intricacies of your arsenal to send those bunnies packing back to their burrows!

An Expansive Arsenal

Your water pistol isn't all you have access to. Throughout the game you'll find multiple upgrades that will provide many interesting solutions to problems. Dash through obstacles with the dash-boots, block paths with the porta-box, and many more secrets waiting for you to uncover.

Dark Secrets

Underneath the surface, darker secrets lie. What really happened to the garden? What about your parents? Find secret items, lore, and bosses using your own curiosity. Maybe then, you can find the secret of the Bun Bun Burrows...
