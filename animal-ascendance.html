Animal Ascendance

A Wild Incremental Game

Increment your Power, Defense, and Ascension with the help of the Animal Ascendance, a powerful group of animals determined to help all reach apotheosis. Plan how you use your resources like land, gold, and most importantly, time.

Indepth Mechanics

While it may be simple to get into, the many mechanics of Animal Ascendance and their overlap can create quite complicated systems to navigate. Players will need to plan with every mechanic in mind in order to optimize gameplay.
